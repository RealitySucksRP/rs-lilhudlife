RS HUDLife — Installation
=========================

1. Extract the rs-lilhudlife folder into your FiveM resources directory.
2. Add "ensure rs-lilhudlife" to your server.cfg (without quotation marks).
3. Set Config.Framework = 'auto' or your chosen framework in config.lua.
4. Restart the resource and open the HUD settings with F9.
5. Validate NUI focus, vehicle transitions, and any optional framework providers on your server.

This is an unencrypted full-source release. See README.txt and LICENSE.
